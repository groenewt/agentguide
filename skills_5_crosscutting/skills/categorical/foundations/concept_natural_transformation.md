# Concept: Natural Transformation — 2-Cell Between Functors
## α:F⇒G assigns αₐ:F(A)→G(A). Naturality: G(f)∘αₐ=αₑ∘F(f). Natural iso if every αₐ is iso.
## Application: Schema evolution = nat. transf. between old and new inter-pillar functors.
## Sources: Mac Lane (1971) Ch.I§4; Eilenberg & Mac Lane (1945) Trans.AMS 58:231–294.
