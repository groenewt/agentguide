# Concept: Limit and Colimit
## Limit: lim D with projections π, universal. Product, Pullback, Equalizer.
## Colimit: colim D with injections ι, universal. Coproduct, Pushout, Coequalizer.
## PyBrain: Product=frozen dataclass, Coproduct=Result, Colimit=Communication, Limit=TierNegotiator.
## Sources: Mac Lane (1971) Ch.III§4, Ch.V; Spivak (2014) §6.1.
